# Request sUAS Flight Operations

Apex Vector LLC coordinates commercial drone data acquisition missions throughout **Reno County, Kansas, and all surrounding regional territories**. Please use the operational metrics below to request a mission profile, structural tracking quote, or aerial mapping consultation.

---

## Operational Intake Brief

To initiate a comprehensive airspace validation and safety review under 14 C.F.R. Part 107 guidelines, please prepare the following parameters for our team:

1. **Target Location:** Geographic coordinates, physical parcel address, or proximity markers relative to Kansas infrastructure.
2. **Asset Profile:** Description of target assets (e.g., concrete silo complexes, industrial line grids, structural tracking configurations).
3. **Data Requirements:** Desired deliverables, such as high-resolution inspection imagery grids, 2D orthomosaic basemaps, or full 3D structural models.
