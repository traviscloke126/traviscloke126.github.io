# Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions farmers, landowners, and businesses ask most. Don't see yours? <a href="#" class="trigger-quote-modal">Send a question</a> and you'll get an answer directly.

## Service & Area

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Where do you fly?</summary>
<p>Apex Vector is based in Hutchinson and serves Reno County and the surrounding counties of central Kansas. Travel within Reno County is included in every quote; jobs outside the county include a travel charge, quoted up front.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What services do you offer?</summary>
<p>Multispectral crop-health mapping and field mapping, structure and roof inspections, construction and site mapping, and aerial photos and 4K video. Ag mapping is the specialty. See <a href="{{ '/operations.html' | relative_url }}">Flight Operations</a> for how each job is run.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Are you licensed and insured?</summary>
<p>Every commercial flight is flown under FAA Part 107 rules by the Remote Pilot in Command, with drone liability insurance in place for the job. Proof of insurance is available on request before your flight.</p>
</details>

## Crop-Health Mapping

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What do I get from a crop-health flight?</summary>
<p>A high-resolution map of the whole field plus NDVI and NDRE crop-health maps from the multispectral camera. These show stress, stand gaps, and drainage problems before they're easy to see from the ground. Zone maps are delivered spray-ready, in the file format your agronomist or spray operator already uses.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Do you spray, or set my application rates?</summary>
<p>No. Apex Vector supplies the data: maps, zones, and suggested spots to ground-truth or soil sample. You and your agronomist decide what to do and set the rates. If you use a spray drone operator, the zone files are built to hand straight to them.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>How do I receive my maps?</summary>
<p>As a download, or on a USB drive ready for your cab. Sales tax may apply to physical copies such as USB drives or prints.</p>
</details>

## Pricing & Payment

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>How much does it cost?</summary>
<p>Ag mapping is priced per acre, inspections per structure, and media by the hour, with a minimum charge for small jobs. Use the estimator on <a href="{{ '/pricing.html' | relative_url }}">Rates &amp; Options</a> for a ballpark. Your final quote is confirmed after an airspace review and site survey.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>How does payment work?</summary>
<p>Jobs are billed in three steps: 30% when you sign the agreement, 40% when the flying is done, and 30% when your files are ready. Your files are released once the final payment clears.</p>
</details>

## Scheduling & Weather

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>What happens if it's too windy?</summary>
<p>This is Kansas, so wind decides a lot. The pilot has final say on whether it's safe to fly. If wind, weather, or visibility rules out your date, the flight is moved to the next good window. Flight dates are confirmed 24 to 48 hours ahead, worked around your schedule.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Do I need to be there on flight day?</summary>
<p>Not necessarily. Before the first flight we walk the site together to note the launch spot, power lines, livestock, and other hazards. On flight day you're welcome to watch from outside the 25-foot safety boundary.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Can you fly near the Hutchinson airport?</summary>
<p>Often, yes. Flights in controlled airspace near Hutchinson Regional Airport (KHUT) require FAA authorization, which is requested and confirmed before your flight is scheduled.</p>
</details>

## Data & Privacy

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Who can see my field data?</summary>
<p>You. Your data is never sold or shared with marketers. It is kept in encrypted storage and shared only with your consent. Your images are used publicly, for example on this website, only if you give permission. Full details are in the <a href="{{ '/privacy.html' | relative_url }}">Privacy Policy</a>.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq-item">
<summary>Will the drone bother my neighbors or livestock?</summary>
<p>Flight plans stay on the authorized property and respect property lines, neighbors' privacy, and livestock. Cameras are not aimed at neighboring homes or people.</p>
</details>
