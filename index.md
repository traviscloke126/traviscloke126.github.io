Based in Reno County, Kansas, **Apex Vector LLC** delivers industrial-grade aerial intelligence. ## Core Capabilities Matrix

* **Industrial Infrastructure Inspection:** Sub-centimeter structural scanning of utility configurations, processing plants, and critical assets.
* **Asset Data Acquisition:** High-resolution 2D and 3D orthomosaic mapping optimized for grain elevators, silos, and agricultural operations.
* **Regulatory Flight Execution:** 100% compliant operations within the National Airspace System (NAS) utilizing real-time LAANC profiles and risk mitigation.

## The Aviation Maintenance Advantage

We do not treat sUAS units like consumer electronics. Our fleet operations are managed under the same rigorous standards that keep heavy-lift military aircraft mission-ready:

1. **Pre-Flight Site Control:** Deployment of an active site safety zone including an inner 15ft critical exclusion safety barrier and an outer 25ft security boundary before any rotor arms.
2. **Component Lifecycle Tracking:** Every propeller, airframe fastener, and LiPo power cell undergoes strict pre- and post-flight inspections modeled directly on Army aviation maintenance regimes.
3. **SOP-Driven Execution:** Zero non-operational communication during critical takeoff, automated grid mapping initialization, and landing phases ensures uncompromised situational awareness.
