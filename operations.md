# Commercial sUAS Operations & Data Capabilities

Apex Vector LLC executes precise data acquisition missions within the United States National Airspace System (NAS). Our services are engineered specifically for commercial agriculture, heavy industry, and structural engineering compliance across Reno County and the surrounding regions.

---

## Technical Capabilities Matrix

### 1. Industrial Infrastructure Tracking
* **Structural Scanning:** Sub-centimeter close-proximity imaging of telecommunication grids, industrial truss configurations, and transmission assets.
* **Pipeline Monitoring:** High-resolution optical surveillance and tracking arrays mapping critical corridor pathways.

### 2. High-Resolution Asset Mapping
* **2D/3D Orthomosaic Models:** Complete aerial data generation tailored for large-scale grain elevators, grain storage facilities, and industrial manufacturing plants.
* **Topographical Mapping:** Accurate grid imagery tracking volume calculations for agricultural stockpile management and land boundaries.

---

## The Operational Environment

All flight operations are conducted strictly inside the operational boundaries established by 14 C.F.R. § Part 107. 

* **Maximum Altitude Profiles:** Missions are locked at or below 400 feet AGL unless executing structural scans with valid regulatory clearances.
* **Automated Flight Execution:** Grid tracking utilizes advanced waypoint flight lines for repeatable, millimeter-precise data compilation over time.
