## 3-Phase Milestone Payment Structure
To secure scheduling and guarantee deliverables, all contracts require three-checkbox client acceptance via our DocuSign Master Pack (SOP-AGREE-003) before work begins:

| Phase | Milestone | Payment Percentage | Trigger Condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1** | **Mobilization** | **30%** | Required to lock flight date and initiate airspace authorizations. |
| **Phase 2** | **In-Field Capture** | **40%** | Due upon successful completion of on-site data acquisition. |
| **Phase 3** | **Output Release** | **30%** | Due prior to the release of finalized processing and data exports. |

*Note: All data operations abide by the Compensable Operations Rule. No raw or processed data will be transferred prior to Phase 3 clearance.*
